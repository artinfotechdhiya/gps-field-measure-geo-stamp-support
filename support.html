App Overview

GPS Field Area Measure - Geo Stamp & Survey is an all-in-one iOS application designed for land surveying, field measurement, photo geo-tagging, and outdoor navigation. It enables land owners, surveyors, farmers, civil engineers, and outdoor enthusiasts to measure field boundaries, calculate perimeters, stamp photos with real-time GPS metadata, and use precision tools like a digital compass, speedometer, and spirit level.

Key Features

GPS Field Area Measurement

Measure land area, perimeter, and distance on live maps by walking along boundaries or selecting points. Supports regional units including Acres, Hectares, Square Feet, Square Meters, Marla, Bigha, and Guntha.

Geo Stamp Camera

Capture photos and videos with GPS coordinates, address, timestamp, altitude, and customizable field notes for site documentation.

Digital Compass

Use the magnetic compass, true-north direction, magnetic declination, and altitude information for field navigation.

GPS Speedometer

Track velocity in km/h, mph, or knots with maximum speed, average speed, and distance metrics.

Spirit Level & Surface Tool

Measure surface inclination, angles, and flatness using the dual-axis bubble level.

Troubleshooting

GPS or measurements are inaccurate

  • Use the app outdoors with a clear view of the sky.
  • Enable Location Services for the app in Settings.
  • Walk the boundary or route steadily and allow the location indicator to stabilize.
  • GPS measurements are estimates and should not replace a professional survey.

Photos or videos do not include a GPS stamp

  • Allow Camera, Photos, and Location access when prompted.
  • Check permissions in Settings > Privacy & Security.
  • Close and reopen the app after changing permissions.

Exports or saved measurements are missing

  • Open My Creations and check the relevant category.
  • Use Share to save or send the exported file.
  • Make sure your device has enough available storage.

To provide measurement and location-stamping features, the app requests the following permissions:

Required

Optional

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: How accurate is the GPS field area measurement?

A: Accuracy depends on your device's GPS signal and the surrounding environment. For best results, use the app outdoors with a clear view of the sky.

Q: Does the app work offline?

A: GPS area measurement, Geo Stamp photo stamping, compass, speedometer, and spirit level features use device hardware and can work without an active internet connection. Map availability may depend on previously loaded map data.

Q: How can I change measurement units?

A: Change the unit system from the app Settings or from the unit selector on the Area Measurement screen.

Q: What should I do if the compass needs calibration?

A: Move the device in a figure-eight motion in open air, away from large metallic or magnetic objects.
