GPS Field Area Measure

Geo Stamp & Surveying Application Information & Support

App Overview

GPS Field Area Measure - Geo Stamp & Survey is an all-in-one iOS application designed for land surveying, field measurement, photo geo-tagging, and outdoor navigation. It enables land owners, surveyors, farmers, civil engineers, and outdoor enthusiasts to measure field boundaries, calculate perimeters, stamp photos with real-time GPS metadata, and utilize precision tools like a digital compass, speedometer, and spirit level.

Key Features

GPS Field Area Measurement

Measure land area, perimeter, and distance on live maps by walking along boundaries or selecting points. Supports conversion across multiple regional land units including Acres, Hectares, Square Feet, Square Meters, Marla, Bigha, and Guntha.

Geo Stamp Camera

Capture photos embedded with automatic watermarks including latitude/longitude coordinates, street address, timestamp, altitude, and custom field notes for official site documentation.

Digital Compass

High-accuracy magnetic compass providing true north direction, magnetic declination, and real-time altitude for field navigation.

GPS Speedometer

Real-time velocity tracking supporting km/h, mph, and knots with maximum speed, average speed, and total distance metrics.

Spirit Level & Surface Tool

Dual-axis bubble level tool to measure surface inclination, angles, and flatness for construction and field setup.

App Permissions Used

To provide high-accuracy measurement and location watermarking, the app requests the following device permissions:

Location Access Required
Used to calculate GPS field area boundaries, determine live latitude and longitude coordinates, measure movement distance, and stamp precise location details onto Geo Stamp photos. Location data is processed locally on your device.
Camera Access Optional
Used solely for taking photos within the Geo Stamp Camera feature to overlay location coordinates, timestamps, and survey tags directly onto captured images.
Photo Library Access Optional
Required to save captured Geo Stamp photos to your iOS photo album and allow importing existing photos for timestamping or exporting survey measurements.
Motion & Gyroscope Sensors Optional
Utilized by the Digital Compass and Spirit Level tools to calculate precise magnetic heading, device orientation, and surface tilt angles.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: How accurate is the GPS field area measurement?
A: Measurement accuracy depends on your device's GPS signal. For best results, use the app in open outdoor environments with a clear view of the sky. Precision is typically within ±0.5 to 2 meters.
Q: Does the app work offline without internet?
A: Yes. GPS area measurement, Geo Stamp photo stamping, compass, speedometer, and spirit level features function offline using hardware sensors. Maps will load cached tiles if opened previously.
Q: Is my location data shared with third parties?
A: No. All location coordinates and photo metadata are generated and saved locally on your device. Your location history is not sold or tracked by third parties.
Q: How can I change measurement units (e.g., Acres to Hectares)?
A: You can switch unit systems at any time from the app Settings or directly from the Area Measurement screen by tapping the unit selector menu.
Q: What should I do if the compass needs calibration?
A: Move your device in a figure-8 motion in open air away from large metallic or magnetic objects to recalibrate the internal magnetometer.
